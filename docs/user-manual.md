# Car2ne user manual

> Intended as a knowledge base: it can be published as the site's "Help / FAQ" section and used as the source for a support chatbot.
> Language: English. The site is bilingual IT/EN — the Italian version lives in [manuale-utente.md](manuale-utente.md) and the two must be kept in sync.

## 1. What Car2ne is

Car2ne is a platform that connects people attending the **same event** (concerts, festivals, fairs, sports, shows) so they can share a car ride and **split the costs**.

Key points:

- Car2ne **does not organize transportation** and **does not handle any payment**: it only connects drivers and passengers. All arrangements (financial and otherwise) are made directly between users.
- The cost contribution is meant to **split expenses** (fuel and tolls), not to make a profit. The platform prevents you from setting a contribution that would generate a profit.
- Car2ne is a **non-profit** project. No ads, no data selling, no commission taken.
- Reserved for people **aged 18 or older**.
- Every published ride is **round trip**.

Contacts:

- Problems, reports, misconduct: **report@car2ne.com** or the *Report a problem* page.
- Privacy and personal data: **privacy@car2ne.com**.
- Social: Instagram and TikTok **@car2ne_official**.

## 2. Account

### 2.1 Sign up

Two ways:

1. **Email and password** — name, surname, date of birth, email and password are required.
2. **Google** — sign in with your Google account; the date of birth is requested right after your first sign-in.

Requirements:

- You must be **at least 18 years old**. Entering a date of birth below 18 means the account is not created.
- You must accept the **Terms and Conditions** and the **Privacy Policy**, otherwise sign-up stays blocked.
- The password must contain: at least 8 characters, at least one number, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one special character.

After signing up with email you receive a **6-digit verification code** by email: enter it to activate the account. The code can be resent after a short cooldown.

### 2.2 Sign in

- Sign in with email and password, or "Continue with Google".
- "Remember me" keeps the session active.
- If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, an extra code is requested after email and password.

### 2.3 Forgot password

On the *Forgot password* page you enter your email; if an account exists for it you receive a **code by email** to set a new password.

### 2.4 Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Optional, enabled from *My profile*:

1. Scan a QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.), or enter the manual code.
2. Confirm by entering the code generated by the app.

On your next sign-in you'll be asked for the 6-digit code. Ticking **"Don't ask me again on this device for 14 days"** skips the code for two weeks on the same device/browser.

To disable it, go back to the profile: an app code is required as identity confirmation before it is switched off. You get a confirmation email when 2FA is enabled and when it is disabled.

### 2.5 My profile

On the *My profile* page you manage:

- **Account information**: name, surname, email — shown but not editable from this page.
- **Public profile**: city, bio, profile photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, max 2 MB). This information can be seen by other users when you offer or book a ride.
- **Payment methods**: PayPal.me, Revolut.me and Satispay links, to make it easier to be paid the cost contribution. Car2ne never sees, handles or holds the money.
- **Password**: change password. Users who signed up with Google can also set a password for email sign-in.
- **Two-factor authentication**: see above.
- **Push notifications**: enable/disable on the current device.
- **Blocked users**: list of people you have blocked, with the option to unblock them.

### 2.6 Blocking a user

From another user's public profile you can use **"Block user"**. From that moment:

- you can no longer exchange messages;
- you can no longer book rides with each other.

You can unblock at any time from your own profile.

### 2.7 Deleting your account

From the profile, *Delete account* section. To confirm you must type the word **DELETE**.

What happens:

- Personal data (name, photo, city, bio) is **removed/anonymized**.
- You will no longer be able to sign in with that account.
- Your **ride and review history stays visible** to other users, but in anonymized form, so linked information isn't broken.
- You receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

This action is **irreversible**.

## 3. Finding an event

- **From the homepage**: search bar for event name and departure city.
- **Events page**: full list with free-text search (event, city, venue) and filters by **city**, **venue** and **searched departure city**, with pagination ("Previous" / "Next").
- **City pages** (`/citta`): events city by city.
- **Venue pages**: all events at a specific venue.
- **Artist pages**: all dates for an artist.

### 3.1 Concluded event

If an event has already taken place, you can no longer search or offer rides for it. You can still **leave a review** for the people you travelled with.

### 3.2 Event not listed

If the event isn't there, use **"Suggest an event"**: you must be registered and paste the link to the event's official page. If the team approves it, the event is added to the Car2ne catalogue.

### 3.3 Following an event with no rides (watchlist)

On an event that has no rides yet you can tap **"Notify me when there's a ride"**. You get a notification as soon as someone publishes a ride for that event. Followed events are in the dashboard, **Followed events** section, where you can also stop following them.

## 4. Offering a ride (driver)

From the **"Offer a ride"** entry.

You can fill in the form **without signing in**: your data isn't lost, sign-in is only requested when you publish.

Fields:

- **Event** — chosen from a list with search by title, artist or city. Venue and date are set automatically by the event.
- **Departure and return city** — the same city applies to both the outbound and return trip.
- **Outbound time** — expected departure time towards the event.
- **Return time** — expected time you leave the event.
- **Available seats**.
- **Contribution per passenger (€)** — per-person amount for the round trip.
- **Description** (optional) — useful information for passengers.

Contribution rules:

- Car2ne suggests an amount in line with carpooling, based on the estimated distance.
- If you enter a contribution that's too high for the distance, a **non-blocking warning** appears ("contribution above the fair share"): you can still publish.
- Above a certain **maximum** the ride would generate a profit and publishing is **blocked**: Car2ne is for splitting costs, not making money.

Other notes:

- You can have **only one active ride per event**. If one already exists, you can edit or delete it from *My rides*.

### 4.1 Managing a published ride

In the dashboard, **My rides** section:

- Ride status: *Active* or *Cancelled*.
- **Manage / Edit**: you can update departure city, times, available seats, contribution and description. Event, destination and date **cannot be changed** (they come from the event).

### 4.2 Booking requests

Interested passengers send a request that **must be confirmed by the driver**. On the ride management page:

- **Accept** — the seat is confirmed (not possible if there are no available seats).
- **Reject** — with an in-page confirmation.
- When a request arrives you get an **in-app notification**.
- After acceptance, the **chat** with the confirmed passenger opens.

### 4.3 Cancelling a ride

From the ride management page, with a **two-step confirmation**. Passengers who had a request or a booking receive a **notification and an email** about the cancellation. The ride is no longer visible or bookable.

## 5. Booking a ride (passenger)

On an event page, **"Available rides"** section:

- For each ride you see the driver (with a **Verified** badge if applicable), outbound and return times, available seats, contribution and — if enabled — the map with the **meeting point**.
- You tap **"Request round trip"** (rides are always outbound + return).
- You cannot request a seat on your own ride.

Request states:

- **Waiting for driver confirmation** — request sent.
- **Seat confirmed** — the driver accepted.
- **Rejected** — you can send a new request ("Request again").

### 5.1 My bookings

In the dashboard, **My bookings** section:

- Status: *Confirmed* or *Cancelled*.
- **Open chat** with the driver (after confirmation).
- **Cancel booking** — the seat becomes available again for others.
- **Pay the driver** — buttons to PayPal, Revolut or Satispay (if the driver added the links) or **pay in person** in cash at the meeting point.

If the driver cancels the ride, the passenger receives a **notification and an email** and the booking is clearly marked as cancelled in the dashboard.

### 5.2 Payments

- Payment happens **directly between passenger and driver**. Car2ne does not step in, does not collect money and does not guarantee the transaction.
- The payment links are a convenience: responsibility for the arrangement lies with the users.

## 6. Chat

- The conversation appears **after a booking is confirmed** (one chat per confirmed booking).
- Messages are **real time**. Send with Enter, new line with Shift + Enter.
- A **floating chat widget** is available on every page, in addition to the full *Chat* page.
- The chat **closes** when the booking is no longer active (e.g. cancelled), when the event has passed, or if one of the two users has blocked the other. In that case no new messages can be sent.
- Use the chat only to arrange the ride: **no spam, advertising or illegal content**.

---

## 7. Notifications

Three channels:

1. **In-app notification centre** (bell): booking requests, confirmations, rejections, ride cancellations, review reminders, unread messages. You can mark them all as read.
2. **Transactional emails** — sent in the **language set on the site**.
3. **Browser push notifications** (optional) — alerts even when Car2ne is not open. Enabled from the profile or the dedicated prompt. If they were blocked at browser level, re-enable them from the browser settings.

---

## 8. Reviews

- After a concluded ride you get a **reminder** to leave a review, both for the driver and for the passenger.
- A review is a **1 to 5 star** rating plus an **optional comment**.
- Received reviews appear on the user's **public profile** and in the *Reviews received* section of your own profile.
- Reviews must be honest: fake, offensive or retaliatory reviews are not allowed.
- If the review author later deleted their account, they are shown as "Car2ne user".

## 9. Verified driver

**Optional** verification that grants the **"Verified driver"** badge on the profile and rides, to increase trust.

What you need (*Driver verification* page in the dashboard):

- Vehicle make, model and **plate**.
- **Driving license number**.
- A **document** (driving license or ID card): JPG, PNG or PDF, max 5 MB.

How the document is handled:

- Stored in a private, access-restricted archive.
- **Deleted right after review**, or automatically **after 14 days** if the request stays pending. Only the outcome (approved/rejected) is kept afterwards.

States: *Under review*, *Verified*, *Rejected*, *Expired* (not reviewed in time — you can resubmit).

Important: the badge only certifies that the submitted documents were reviewed and found consistent. It is **not a guarantee by Car2ne** of the driver's conduct or of the vehicle's condition.

## 10. Safety and community rules

- Car2ne connects people but is **not present during the ride**: use common sense, share your trip details with someone you trust, know the details of the person you're travelling with before departure.
- **Mutual respect** in chat, reviews and during the ride. Offensive language, discrimination, harassment and intimidating behaviour are forbidden.
- **Honest information**: times, seats, city and contribution must be real and up to date. Fake rides are forbidden.
- Forbidden: fake or multiple accounts, fraudulent behaviour, advertising of services unrelated to Car2ne, illegal content or content infringing others' rights.
- Violating the Terms or the Community Guidelines can lead to **account suspension**.

### 10.1 Reporting a problem

The **"Report a problem"** page (or email **report@car2ne.com**). Categories:

- User behaviour
- Inappropriate content
- Technical issue
- Safety
- No-show
- Other

To help us respond faster: a clear description, the event/date and the other user's name if relevant, any screenshots. Updates on the report arrive via notifications.

### 10.2 No-show

If the other party doesn't show up at the meeting point you can use **"Report no-show"** from the ride/booking, with an optional note. The team reviews the report.

## 12. FAQ

### General

**Is Car2ne free?**
Yes. There are no sign-up fees and no commission. The only money involved is the cost contribution the passenger pays directly to the driver.

**Does Car2ne take a cut of the ride?**
No. Car2ne does not handle or process any payment. The money goes directly between passenger and driver.

**Who can use Car2ne?**
Anyone aged 18 or older.

**Which cities is it available in?**
Car2ne covers events all over Italy. You can search by departure city and by event city.

**Can I use Car2ne in English?**
Yes, the site is bilingual Italian/English. Switch language from the selector at the top; emails follow the chosen language too.

### Account

**How do I sign up?**
With email and password, or with Google. In both cases you provide your date of birth (18+) and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy.

**I didn't receive the email verification code.**
Check your spam folder. You can request a new code from the verification page after a few seconds' cooldown.

**I forgot my password.**
Go to "Forgot password", enter your email and you'll get a code to set a new one.

**I signed up with Google — can I also have a password?**
Yes. In the profile, Password section, you can set a password for email sign-in.

**How do I enable two-factor authentication?**
From the profile: scan the QR code with an app like Google Authenticator or Authy and confirm the code. At sign-in you can choose not to repeat the check on the same device for 14 days.

**Can I change my name, surname or email?**
Not by yourself from the profile page. Write to report@car2ne.com if you need them corrected.

**How do I delete my account?**
From the profile, "Delete account" section: type "DELETE" to confirm. Your personal data is anonymized; rides and reviews stay visible in anonymized form. The action is irreversible.

**What happens to my data if I delete my account?**
Name, photo, city and bio are removed. You can no longer sign in. History linked to other users remains, but without your identifying data.

### Events

**I can't find my event.**
Use "Suggest an event" and paste the link to the official page. If approved, it's added to the catalogue. You need to be registered.

**The event has passed — can I still do anything?**
You can no longer search or offer rides, but you can leave a review for the people you travelled with.

**How do I know if someone publishes a ride for an event?**
Open the event and tap "Notify me when there's a ride". You'll get a notification as soon as a ride is published.

### Offering a ride

**Do I have to be registered to fill in the form?**
No, you can fill it in freely. Sign-in is only requested when you publish, and your data isn't lost.

**How much can I ask as a contribution?**
An amount in line with splitting the expenses (fuel + tolls) for the round trip. The system suggests an amount based on distance and blocks publishing if the contribution becomes a profit.

**Can I publish more than one ride for the same event?**
No, only one per event. You can edit or delete it from "My rides".

**Can I edit an already published ride?**
Yes: departure city, times, seats, contribution and description. You can't change the event, destination and date.

**Do I have to accept passengers myself?**
Yes. Every booking request must be confirmed (or rejected) by you. You get a notification when a request arrives.

**What happens if I cancel the ride?**
A two-step confirmation is required. All passengers with a request or a booking receive a notification and an email.

**Do I need to be a "verified driver" to offer rides?**
No, verification is optional. It only shows a trust badge.

### Booking a ride

**How do I book?**
Open the event, choose a ride and tap "Request round trip". The driver must confirm.

**Can I book only the outbound or only the return trip?**
No, rides on Car2ne are always round trip.

**How long does the driver take to confirm?**
It depends on the driver. Until they confirm, the request stays "pending".

**My request was rejected.**
You can send a new one with "Request again", or look for another ride.

**How do I pay the driver?**
From the "My bookings" section, with the PayPal / Revolut / Satispay buttons (if the driver set them up) or in cash in person. Payment is always directly between you and the driver.

**I booked but I can no longer go.**
Cancel the booking from "My bookings": the seat becomes available again for others.

**The driver cancelled the ride.**
You get a notification and an email. The booking is marked as cancelled in your dashboard and you can look for another ride.

### Chat

**I can't message the driver / the passenger.**
The chat only becomes active after the booking is confirmed. If it was active and is now closed, it may be because the booking was cancelled, the event has passed, or there's a block between users.

**Where do I find my conversations?**
On the "Chat" page or in the floating chat widget available on every page.

### Notifications

**I'm not receiving emails.**
Check your spam folder. Emails arrive in the language set on the site. If the problem persists, report it to report@car2ne.com.

**How do I enable push notifications?**
From the profile or the dedicated prompt. If you blocked them in the browser, re-enable them in the browser settings for the Car2ne site.

### Reviews

**When can I leave a review?**
After the event has taken place. You get a reminder in your notifications.

**Can I edit or remove a review?**
Reviews reflect the experience you had. For offensive or false content, report it via "Report a problem".

### Safety

**Does Car2ne vouch for the driver or the condition of the car?**
No. Car2ne connects people but is not present during the ride. The "verified driver" badge only certifies that the submitted documents were checked.

**I had a problem with another user.**
Use "Report a problem" (or report@car2ne.com). You can also block the user from their public profile.

**The other person didn't show up.**
Use "Report no-show" from the ride or the booking.

### Privacy

**What data does Car2ne collect?**
Registration data, date of birth (only to verify you are 18+), optional profile data, ride and booking data, reviews, chat messages, any driver verification data, technical data. No advertising profiling.

**Does Car2ne use tracking cookies?**
No, only technical cookies necessary for the service to work.

**How do I exercise my data rights?**
Write to privacy@car2ne.com for access, rectification, erasure, objection or portability.
